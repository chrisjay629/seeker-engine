**PATTERN**

Records show that several deaths have documented, unrelated causes: Michael David Hicks died of arteriosclerotic cardiovascular disease with morbid obesity, Amy Eskridge died by suicide, and Joshua LeBlanc died in a traffic accident. Two cases, Carl Grillmair and Nuno Loureiro, are prosecuted homicides. The death of Melissa Casias involved a gunshot wound, but authorities have not officially determined the manner of death. Frank Maiwald and Matthew James Sullivan died without publicly disclosed causes. Four individuals remain missing: William Neil McCasland, Monica Jacinto Reza, Anthony Chavez, and Steven Garcia. Authorities have not found evidence of a systemic threat. A claim circulates that these cases are linked by a coordinated pattern of targeting; however, this claim is unproven.
